# Hi! I'm Pol Cárceles 👋

### Electronics Engineering Student | Firmware and Hardware Development

I enjoy bridging the gap between hardware and software—designing custom PCBs, writing low-level firmware, and building reliable embedded systems from scratch.

---

## 🚀 About Me

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Developing a custom **STM32-based MP3 Audio Player** (PCB routing in KiCad & modular C firmware) and researching low-cost absolute encoders at **UPC (Moonshot)**.
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Advanced STM32 peripheral optimization and FPGA design with VHDL.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Embedded C, microcontroller interfacing (STM32/Arduino), schematic capture/PCB design in KiCad, or hardware validation.
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I love taking a project all the way from a breadboard prototype and 3D-printed enclosure to a finished PCB.
